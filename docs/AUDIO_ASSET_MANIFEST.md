# Audio Asset and Evidence Manifest

This manifest pins every non-procedural audio source in the build. Runtime
music is Ogg Opus and sampled effects are Ogg Vorbis; no MIDI file, soundfont,
General MIDI renderer, network stream, or runtime-generated melody is used.

The three complete compositions replace the former 12.9–36.9-second loop set.
Menu, ordinary battle, and boss each have a separate musical identity. Pressure
changes add restrained synchronized percussion and low-register accents over
the continuously running battle composition; they never seek or restart it.
Boss arrival performs one explicit crossfade into the separate boss work and is
paired with a critical sting and battlefield shock. Victory stops the boss
program beneath its fanfare instead of forcing a second transition.

The runtime includes three variants for each ordinary family:

- system: click, invalid, confirmation, selection;
- materials: wood, metal, glass, plate, bell;

Files are under `public/assets/audio/sfx/{ui,impact,rpg}`. Selection is
deterministic round-robin, preserving repeatable simulation behavior while
preventing identical consecutive transients.

## Source acceptance decision

Accepted assets have an explicit, auditable grant and source hash. Music is
CC BY 4.0 with visible runtime attribution; sampled effects are CC0.
Ambiguous sample provenance and free-tier-only commercial terms were rejected.
In particular, GeneralUser GS was not accepted because its own documentation
flags uncertainty around some underlying sample permissions. Generative-music
services were not used because their free tiers or ownership terms did not
provide a cleaner provenance chain than these pinned CC0 sources.

## Generated evidence

The 90-second browser capture and analysis are produced by
`pnpm capture:audio`. Outputs:

- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s.webm`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s.wav`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s-spectrogram.png`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s-loudness.txt`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s-diagnostics.json`

The loudness report is generated by FFmpeg `ebur128`; the image is generated
from the same decoded capture, so evidence and analyzed program cannot diverge.
Regenerate these files after any score or mixer change. The current 90.36-second
browser master measures −15.4 LUFS-I, 15.5 LU LRA, and −1.0 dBTP. Its
transport evidence reports zero failed assets, 48 decoded short samples, three
streamed music programs, zero decoded music bytes, two intentional program
changes, and zero mid-phrase restarts. Battle's playhead advances continuously
from 0.08 to 20.18 to 45.25 seconds through pressure changes. At boss arrival,
the battle source remains alive until the cold boss stream emits `playing`; the
captured source-stop count advances only afterward. `silencedetect` finds no
interval at or below −42 dB for 250 ms anywhere in the complete capture.
