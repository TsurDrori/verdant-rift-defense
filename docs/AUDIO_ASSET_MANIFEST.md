# Audio Asset and Evidence Manifest

This manifest pins every non-procedural audio source in the build. Runtime
music and sampled effects are OGG Vorbis; no MIDI file, soundfont, General MIDI
renderer, network stream, or runtime-generated melody is used.

The source package contains compound alternatives as well as loops. Decoded
PCM comparison confirms that `Intro2.ogg` shares the full 25.846-second
`Intro1.ogg` prefix, `Transi2to3.ogg` is phase-compatible with the complete
14.769-second `Loop2.ogg`, and `Loop3End.ogg` shares the complete 12.923-second
`Loop3.ogg` prefix. `AudioDirector` transfers those pairs at the current source
offset. It does not concatenate them from zero in the middle of a phrase.

Calm and active requests use dwell-gated horizontal resequencing; crisis adds a
restrained synchronized layer over the uninterrupted active loop; boss adopts
the supplied transition at the active playhead; and victory adopts the supplied
ending at the boss-loop playhead. Attribution, archives, hashes, and the CC0
license records above are unchanged.

The runtime includes three variants for each ordinary family:

- system: click, invalid, confirmation, selection;
- materials: wood, metal, glass, plate, bell;
- body weights: medium punch, heavy punch, medium soft, heavy soft;
- weapons/props: blade, creak, metal vessel.

Files are under `public/assets/audio/sfx/{ui,impact,rpg}`. Selection is
deterministic round-robin, preserving repeatable simulation behavior while
preventing identical consecutive transients.

## Source acceptance decision

Accepted assets have an explicit, auditable CC0 grant and source archive hash.
Ambiguous sample provenance and free-tier-only commercial terms were rejected.
In particular, GeneralUser GS was not accepted because its own documentation
flags uncertainty around some underlying sample permissions. Generative-music
services were not used because their free tiers or ownership terms did not
provide a cleaner provenance chain than these pinned CC0 sources.

## Generated evidence

The 90-second browser capture and analysis are produced by
`pnpm capture:audio`. Outputs:

- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s.webm`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s.wav`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s-spectrogram.png`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s-loudness.txt`
- `artifacts/audio-evidence/adaptive-90s-diagnostics.json`

The loudness report is generated by FFmpeg `ebur128`; the image is generated
from the same decoded capture, so evidence and analyzed program cannot diverge.
The current 90.24-second master measures −16.0 LUFS-I, 1.7 LU LRA, and
−1.2 dBTP. The transition-to-boss-loop handoff is pre-scheduled on the
`AudioContext` clock; 85 ms analysis frames spanning the join remain between
−15.4 and −23.1 dB RMS, with no full-band program dropout.
